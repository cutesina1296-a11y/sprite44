Overview

Live account, wallet and operations overview.

Operational safety

Payment requests stay pending until an administrator verifies the payment or payout. Approving a recharge credits the internal wallet; approving a withdrawal deducts it. This panel does not send money or integrate with a payment provider.

Wallet balances are stored server-side in paise. Every admin balance change, request decision and configuration update is recorded in the audit log.

Users

Suspending an account blocks access but preserves its wallet and history; suspended accounts can be reactivated. Wallet credits/debits update the user’s available balance for active product purchases and eligible withdrawal requests. Withdrawals require saved bank details and admin review; adjustments do not send payouts.

Recharge & withdrawal requests

Only approve a recharge after independently confirming receipt. For withdrawals, mark the request Processing while arranging the payout, reveal bank details only when needed, and approve with the payout reference after sending it manually. Processing withdrawals continue to reserve wallet funds. Reveals are audited; this panel does not send money.
